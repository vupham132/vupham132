# Trung Tâm Thanh Hằng

> Full-stack education-center operations platform

[← Back to profile](../README.md) · [View screenshots and workflows](https://phamthanhvu-portfolio.vercel.app/projects/tieng-trung-thanh-hang)

## Overview

An integrated platform connecting customer acquisition, enrollment, academic operations, finance, people operations, inventory, and online learning.

## Product scope

- CRM, leads, consultations, conversion, labels, reminders, and sales assignment
- Courses, enrollments, classes, opening workflows, schedules, rooms, and transfers
- Attendance, grades, exams, deferrals, re-enrollment, and student records
- Orders, receipts, invoices, debt, commissions, promotions, and approvals
- Teachers, recruitment, employee records, leave, evaluations, and payroll support
- Books, warehouses, purchasing, stock transfers, and inventory reporting
- Student portal, teacher workflows, lesson content, assignments, progress, and forums

## Architecture and stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, React Admin, Material UI, Data Grid, FullCalendar
- **Backend:** Laravel, Sanctum, Passport, S3 storage, Excel and Word processing
- **Quality:** Vitest, Playwright, Testing Library, PHPUnit

## Role

**Full-Stack Developer** — working across frontend modules, backend APIs, business rules, authorization, reporting, integrations, and delivery workflows.
