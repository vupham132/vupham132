# Ninh Bình Tourism

> Multilingual tourism discovery and content platform

[← Back to profile](../README.md)

## Overview

A tourism platform that helps visitors discover destinations and guides while providing membership, paid-content, analytics, and administration capabilities.

## Product scope

- Tourism listings, translations, full-text search, filters, and listing details
- Guides, rich-text publishing, itinerary creation, and multilingual content
- Member accounts, search history, purchases, bookings, and paid content
- Payments, email delivery, media processing, and secured file uploads
- Analytics, access/search statistics, reporting, audit logs, and runtime configuration
- Administrative accounts, dashboards, settings, and content management

## Architecture and stack

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, i18next, Markdown/editor tooling, Recharts
- **Backend:** Fastify, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Argon2, Zod, Sharp, Nodemailer
- **Security:** Helmet, rate limiting, CORS, secure cookies, validation, and audit logging

## Role

**Full-Stack Developer** — building user journeys, admin workflows, APIs, database-backed modules, multilingual content, payments, and analytics.
