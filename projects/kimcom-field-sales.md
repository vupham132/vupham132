# Kimcom Field Sales PM

> Full-stack field-sales and project operations platform

[← Back to profile](../README.md)

## Overview

An operations platform for managing field-sales projects from planning and team assignment through execution, evidence collection, inventory, sales, promotions, and reporting.

## Product scope

- Projects, configurations, teams, departments, permissions, and Gantt planning
- Store/region assignment, employee imports, shifts, check-ins, and attendance alerts
- Sampling programs, conversion tracking, KPIs, progress, and shift reports
- Products, stock records, inventory receipts, transactions, and adjustments
- Sales orders, custom sales forms, promotions, gifts, and public prize experiences
- Evidence photos, photo library, audit logs, chat, notifications, archives, and trash recovery
- Dashboards, client reporting, Excel/Word exports, and operational analytics

## Architecture and stack

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Dexie, React Hook Form, Recharts
- **Backend:** Laravel, Sanctum, Reverb, Redis, S3, Spatie Permission and Query Builder
- **Quality:** Vitest and Pest

## Role

**Full-Stack Developer** — implementing connected frontend/backend workflows, business rules, reporting, authorization, media handling, and operational tooling.
