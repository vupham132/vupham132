# IELTS Hana / WA P061

> Full-stack education-management platform

[← Back to profile](../README.md) · [View screenshots and workflows](https://phamthanhvu-portfolio.vercel.app/projects/ielts-hana)

## Overview

A modular platform for managing the academic, operational, and financial workflows of an IELTS education center.

## Product scope

- Students, teachers, staff profiles, departments, roles, and permission matrix
- Course packages, curricula, lessons, class schedules, teaching assignments, and attendance
- Assignments, questions, submissions, grading, placement tests, and evaluations
- Enrollment, tuition plans, installments, payments, receipts, and receiving accounts
- Teacher timesheets, payroll rates, adjustments, and payroll calculation
- Teaching library, learning content, file management, templates, and generated documents
- Dashboards, notifications, audit logs, and data imports

## Architecture and stack

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Material UI, Data Grid, Zustand, Dexie
- **Backend:** Laravel, Sanctum, S3-compatible storage
- **Documents and data:** Excel/Word templates, imports, previews, and generated documents

## Role

**Full-Stack Developer** — delivering academic and finance modules across UI, API, data models, permissions, document workflows, and reporting.
