# Speak With Hằng

> Full-stack English-learning and content platform

[← Back to profile](../README.md) · [View screenshots and workflows](https://phamthanhvu-portfolio.vercel.app/projects/speakwithhang)

## Overview

A customer-facing education product combining course discovery, video-based learning, enrollment and payment flows, content publishing, and student engagement.

## Product scope

- Course catalog, course details, 1-on-1 learning, pronunciation, grammar, and video courses
- Registration, login, password recovery, customer profiles, and student dashboard
- Enrollment, orders, payment completion/failure flows, and webhooks
- Video lessons, submissions, completion tracking, and student feedback
- Blog, categories, tags, custom pages, recruitment, and team content
- Learning roadmaps, notifications, settings, and activity logs

## Architecture and stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI, React Hook Form, Zod
- **Backend:** Laravel, Sanctum, Google API integration, media processing
- **UI and analytics:** Framer Motion, Recharts, reusable component system

## Role

**Full-Stack Developer** — delivering product features across public pages, authenticated learning experiences, CMS workflows, APIs, data models, and integrations.
